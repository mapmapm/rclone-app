Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Rclone Personal Application ("Application") handles your information when you use it.

2. Information Collection

This Application does not collect, store, or process any personal information.

The Application only accesses your Google Drive files through the official Google Drive API with your explicit consent. All file operations are performed directly between your local device and Google's servers.

3. Data Storage

No data is stored by this Application. Your files remain on your local device and/or your Google Drive account. The Application does not maintain any databases, logs, or copies of your data.

4. Third-Party Services

This Application uses only the official Google Drive API. No other third-party services are involved. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google Drive data.

5. Data Sharing

No data is shared with any third parties. The Application is for personal use only and does not transmit any information to external servers or services.

6. Security

The Application uses OAuth 2.0 for authentication, which is the industry standard for secure API access. Your credentials are never stored or transmitted by the Application.

7. Your Rights

Since no data is collected, there is no data to access, modify, or delete through this Application. You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

This Application is not intended for use by anyone and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

This is a personal application. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Application owner through their Google account.
